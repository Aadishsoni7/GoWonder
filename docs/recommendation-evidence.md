# Recommendation journey evidence

Checked 20 October 2026. Module: `src/data/recommendation-people.ts`.

The module exports `recommendationPeople` (Tony Jebara and Justin Basilico) and `recommendationResources` (six resources). Roles are deliberately historical. These pages do not establish current employment or job titles in October 2026.

## Primary evidence

- [Spotify interview, 24 October 2019](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2019-10-24/6-questions-and-answers-with-tony-jebara-vp-of-machine-learning/): Tony studied machine learning in college, spent four years leading machine learning at Netflix and described analysing listening data and running experiments at Spotify. Also directly supports his guitar and songwriting interest.
- [Tony's explanation of Spotify Home, 16 January 2020](https://engineering.atspotify.com/2020/1/for-your-ears-only-personalizing-spotify-home-with-machine-learning): direct work on personalisation algorithms, machine learning, exploration, experiments and data quality.
- [Tony's workday, 18 March 2020](https://engineering.atspotify.com/2020/3/my-beat-tony-jebara): engineering and product conversations, technical strategy and external research speakers. Daily habits are attributed to this dated account.
- [Learning a Personalized Homepage, 9 April 2015](https://netflixtechblog.com/learning-a-personalized-homepage-aa8ec670359a): coauthored by Chris Alvino and Justin Basilico. Directly explains recommendation selection, ranking, machine learning, training data and evaluation.
- [Justin's Scale conference talk, 6 October 2021](https://learn.scale.com/public/videos/trends-in-recommendation-and-personalization-at-netflix): first person talk and transcript describing recommendation research, ranking, data, experiments and measurement.

The seven proposed nodes have direct evidence through these practitioners: recommendation-systems, machine-learning, algorithms, ranking, experimentation, personalization and data-science. Tony is not assigned to ranking separately because Justin's sources support that narrower label more explicitly.
