# GoWonder discovery questionnaire

Version discovery-1. Editorial mapping, not a validated psychological test. See discovery-scoring-review.md for rationale.

## q1. You have a day on a film set. What would you want to understand first?

A. How the cameras and equipment work — Make (R)

B. How the story becomes a scene — Create (A)

C. How the cast and crew work together — Connect (S)

D. How the shoot is scheduled and organised — Find the pattern (C)

## q2. You have ₹1,000 for a student project. What would you rather do?

A. Build something useful — Make (R)

B. Create a short film or artwork — Create (A)

C. Organise the details of a student event — Find the pattern (C)

D. Try selling something people might want — Make it happen (E)

## q3. You come across a game you have never played. What draws you in?

A. Understanding the rules and scoring — Find the pattern (C)

B. Exploring its world and story — Create (A)

C. Playing with other people — Connect (S)

D. Bring players together around a strategy to win — Make it happen (E)

## q4. A friend’s small business is struggling to find customers. What would you try first?

A. Listen to customers and understand their needs — Connect (S)

B. Study the sales numbers for clues — Figure out (I)

C. Create a clearer advertisement — Create (A)

D. Test a new way to attract customers — Make it happen (E)

## q5. You visit a science museum. Which exhibit would you choose first?

A. Machines I can operate and explore — Make (R)

B. Evidence about the mysteries of the universe — Figure out (I)

C. An experiment with a method and results to compare — Find the pattern (C)

D. Stories of how science helps a community — Connect (S)

B. Why a particular strategy works — Figure out (I)
