# Offbeat upgrade, 19 October 2026

This updates the existing React application. Original node IDs, 699 original level tasks, licensed portraits, resources, mini games and storage keys remain intact. Additional group and sport records have their own tasks. No authentication, Supabase, payments or questionnaire is enabled.

## Implemented

- Ten familiar homepage world entrances and a separate Surprise Me entrance.
- Individual curiosity questions for every published possibility. Surprise opens a question in the graph and avoids immediate repetition.
- Question, world, person, resource and possibility cards use distinct presentations. Experiments live inside expanded graph cards.
- Direct Meet a practitioner buttons on selected possibilities.
- Public search and graph links exclude candidates without a direct saved practitioner connection. Useful descendants remain reachable when an unsupported intermediate is withheld.
- A fixed, explicit saved evidence ledger preserves previously researched relationships. Adding an arbitrary URL to a new person does not automatically qualify a node for publication.
- Primary sources revisited for key journeys. Newly researched athletes and a sports science researcher have dated evidence records. Previously researched records are labelled as reused evidence, not newly checked links.
- Sports groupings for precision, water, team and throwing events; football, swimming, rowing, javelin and sports science added with real people and safe introductions.
- Customer success and business software sales have new first person practitioner interviews.
- Sales routes use connected paths rather than falsely representing every role as a subtype of a selling model.
- Rhythm and scene ordering mini experiences, alongside existing sales, ledger, thumbnail, sports, UX and game exercises.
- Larger typography, colourful gradients, floating vector drawings and reduced motion support.
- Saved notes for unpublished candidates remain visible in the notebook and in exported progress. Old candidate links explain why an entry is under review.
