# Offbeat: Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3 content

233 entries and 699 authored task drafts. These are learning experiences, not professional training or competency certification. Time estimates are flexible planning suggestions. Keep the activity in the field card and reveal one level at a time. A person story and external source appear alongside it, not instead of it.

## business

### Business `business`

Notice a need. Make something useful.

**Level 1: Start with what you have**

List three small frustrations from an ordinary school day. Ask a friend which one matters to them.

**Level 2: Practise and make something**

Choose one frustration and sketch a service that could help. Ask three willing people what you misunderstood.

**Level 3: Take it further**

Run a permission based pilot with no payments. Record time, materials, feedback and whether the problem became smaller.

**Reflect:** Did listening change the problem you wanted to solve?

**Tools and access:** A spreadsheet can help compare costs after the paper pilot.

### Sales `sales`

Find out what someone needs and explain what helps.

**Level 1: Start with what you have**

Ask a willing friend how they choose a notebook. Explain one feature that answers their stated need.

**Level 2: Practise and make something**

Roleplay three customers with different needs. Write one useful question and one honest limitation for each.

**Level 3: Take it further**

Build a discovery call and demonstration for a fictional service. Ask a partner to score listening, clarity and fit.

**Reflect:** Did you understand the need before recommending something?

**Tools and access:** A free spreadsheet can track fictional enquiries; a paid CRM is unnecessary.

### Sales engineering `sales-engineering`
