# People B source and portrait ledger

30 profiles. Research checked 17–18 October 2026. No fabricated daily schedules, inferred personal habits or generic substitute faces. Three verified YouTube links are optional. Names are labelled as icons, practitioners or career transitions; familiar people are not presented as hidden gems.

Portrait files are Wikimedia Commons thumbnails, retaining their source licenses. Display crops should carry attribution and the image source link. Each license URL below is part of the attribution record. Aditi Mittal uses a real still from a licensed Commons performance video, and is distant; do not describe this as a close portrait. Raja Kumari and Anoushka Shankar are explicitly labelled diaspora.

## Remaining coverage gaps

No verified portrait-backed specialists added for cubing, UX research, sales engineering, game audio or music supervision. Do not connect adjacent practitioners to these subjects just to make a profile appear. Scope has music, chess, game development, open software, mapping, educational tools, content and business.
